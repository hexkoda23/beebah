Founder & Creative Director

01 — The Woman Behind It

Habibat Sosanya

A creative entrepreneur, an HND student of Mass Communication at Federal Polytechnic, Ilaro — and the founder of a beauty brand built on one idea: that a good install changes how a woman walks into a room.

Her passion runs past the salon chair into media, communication and public relations. Through industrial training at Radio Lagos (Eko FM) in the Administrative and Human Resources department, she sharpened the office administration, teamwork and professional communication skills that now run the business side of the brand.

She is committed to continuous learning, innovation and excellence — with a goal of collaborating with professionals across the world and representing Nigerian talent on an international stage.

02 — What Drives It

Mission

A professional beauty brand specialising in hairstyling, wig installations and hair transformations — committed to enhancing confidence by delivering high-quality, personalised beauty services in a professional and welcoming environment.

Through creativity, attention to detail and excellent customer service, every client should look and feel their best.

Vision

To become a globally recognised beauty brand known for excellence, innovation and professionalism — while empowering individuals through beauty, creating employment opportunities, and representing Nigerian creativity on the international stage.

Beauty as enterprise. Craft as export.
